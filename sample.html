Part 2 — Sample Audit excerpt (from the $395 document)

One finding, worked the way the Audit works it

An Audit finding is a chain, not a comment. Each link is labelled so the owner can challenge it.

Complaint (sample review wording, invented)“Bathrooms were grim by Saturday morning, two showers for the whole floor and neither looked cleaned.”
RecurrenceBathroom cleanliness appears 23 times in the sample set; 17 of the 23 are dated Friday–Sunday stays.
Likely causeThe turnover standard exists as a morning routine sized for weekday occupancy. On full-turnover days the same routine runs at double load with no second pass, so bathrooms degrade by the second morning.
What must be checked with the ownerActual weekend staffing on the rota; whether a midday bathroom pass exists on paper or only in habit; cleaning supply stock on weekends.
Proposed changeA 12-point bathroom turnover checklist with a named second pass at 13:00 on full-turnover days, and a supervisor sign-off photo posted to the staff chat. Cost: printing and one hour of training.
OwnerHousekeeping lead owns the checklist; manager owns the weekend rota line.
Follow-up evidenceRe-count bathroom mentions in reviews dated 30 days after the change, and compare against this baseline of 23. Reported as a count, never as a promised rating.

A sample reply from the Audit's template set

“Thanks for staying with us, and I'm sorry the bathrooms let the weekend down. We've changed how we clean on full-turnover days — there's now a second check at lunchtime, owned by our housekeeping lead. If you pass this way again, I'd rather you judged us on that.” — drafted in the property's voice; the owner approves every reply before it posts.

One thing this Audit would NOT fix yet

The sample reviews also mention thin mattresses (6 mentions). The Audit would list that under “not yet”, on purpose: six mentions against 23 for bathrooms, and mattress replacement is a capital purchase. Fix the free operational problem first, re-measure, then decide whether the mattresses are the next real pattern or background noise. An audit that recommends everything recommends nothing.
